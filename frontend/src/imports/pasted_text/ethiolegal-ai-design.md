Design a complete modern AI SaaS web application UI/UX for a project called “EthioLegal AI” — an AI-powered Ethiopian Legal Assistant platform.

IMPORTANT DESIGN STYLE:

* Professional AI SaaS startup design
* Clean, modern, premium, futuristic
* Minimal but powerful
* Dark mode primary theme
* Mobile responsive
* Accessible UI
* Smooth modern gradients
* African/Ethiopian inspired subtle identity
* Glassmorphism + soft shadows
* Elegant typography
* Modern dashboard layout
* Similar quality level to ChatGPT, Notion AI, Perplexity AI, Stripe Dashboard, and modern AI startups

TARGET USERS:

* Ethiopian citizens
* Students
* Workers
* Tenants
* Small businesses
* People with limited legal knowledge
* Multilingual users (English, Amharic, Afaan Oromo)

PROJECT PURPOSE:
This platform helps Ethiopian people understand legal documents and rights using AI. Users can upload contracts or legal PDFs and ask questions in simple language. The AI explains laws, summarizes contracts, detects risky clauses, and supports local Ethiopian languages.

IMPORTANT:
This is NOT a lawyer replacement. Include subtle disclaimer areas:
“This platform provides educational legal information and not official legal advice.”
